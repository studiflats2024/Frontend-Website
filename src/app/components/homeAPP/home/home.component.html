How StudiFlats works

From Search to Stay, We’ve Got You Covered

Students can set their search criteria in the StudiFlats app to receive personalized apartment suggestions. They can then browse and select the perfect option that fits their needs.

After selecting an apartment, users can confirm their inquiry and upload the necessary documents to sign the lease agreement directly through the app.

The app lets students manage their rental, handle payments, and report issues seamlessly, ensuring a hassle-free rental experience.
